# 🌟 PRESENTATION — EXTASIS DONKER

**Extasis Donker** is the definitive synthesizer meticulously engineered to create the signature **DONK** and LatelyBass sounds. Forget the complexity of traditional multi-operator FM synthesizers; Extasis Donker is built strictly upon the "Sweet Spot" principle, guaranteeing that every adjustment you make sounds musical, hard-hitting, and club-ready.

### 🚀 Key Features:
* **Optimized 2-Operator FM Engine:** Authentically models the gritty, metallic, and elastic timbres of vintage hardware (like the Yamaha TX81Z).
* **Shape Morphing:** Blend seamlessly between Pure Sine (W1), Yamaha Half-Sine (W5), and Full-Wave Rectified (W3) operators.
* **Auto-Sidechain (Auto Pump):** Built-in BPM-synced ducking for instant Guaracha or UK Bounce rhythm, without external routing.
* **Pitch Drop Laser Envelope:** Ultra-fast exponential pitch drops (+0 to 24 semitones) for aggressive transient thuds.
* **Strict Mono Sub-Oscillator:** Dedicated sub-bass generator with harmonic drive, locked to mono to ensure massive phase-aligned low end.
* **Top Spread Stereo Dimension:** Stereo widening applied exclusively to frequencies above 180Hz, keeping the bass foundation solid.
* **Hardware-Modeled FX:** Features 'TX Crunch' (12-bit DAC distortion), 'Erosion' (2.8kHz Ring Mod for small speakers), and 'Punch Slam' (upward/downward OTT compression).
* **Interactive Audition Interface:** A massive central logo pad that lets you trigger notes and drag vertically to transpose pitch in real-time.
* **Dynamic Hover Tooltips:** The vintage green phosphor LCD doubles as an intelligent info-board, explaining every parameter as you hover over it.
* **MIDI Expressiveness:** Full support for Pitch Bend (±2 semitones) and Mod Wheel (dynamically opens the filter up to +200%).
* **Multi-Format Support:** VST3, AU, and Standalone on Windows and macOS.
