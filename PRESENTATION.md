# 🌟 PRESENTATION — EXTASIS DONKER (v4.0.0 Free & Open Source)

**Extasis Donker** is a **100% Free & Open Source (GPLv3)** synthesizer meticulously engineered to create the signature **DONK** and LatelyBass sounds. Forget the complexity of traditional multi-operator FM synthesizers; Extasis Donker is built strictly upon the "Sweet Spot" principle, guaranteeing that every adjustment you make sounds musical, hard-hitting, and club-ready.

### 🚀 Key Features:
* **100% Free & Open Source (GPLv3):** No DRM, no serial keys, no demo timers — completely free forever.
* **Optimized 2-Operator FM Engine:** Authentically models the gritty, metallic, and elastic timbres of vintage hardware (like the Yamaha TX81Z).
* **Shape Morphing:** Blend seamlessly between Pure Sine (W1), Yamaha Half-Sine (W5), and Full-Wave Rectified (W3) operators.
* **Auto-Sidechain (Auto Pump):** Built-in BPM-synced ducking for instant Guaracha or UK Bounce rhythm, without external routing.
* **Pitch Drop Laser Envelope:** Ultra-fast exponential pitch drops (0 to +24 semitones) for aggressive transient thuds.
* **Strict Mono Sub-Oscillator:** Dedicated sub-bass generator with harmonic drive, locked to mono to ensure massive phase-aligned low end.
* **Top Spread Stereo Dimension:** Linearly interpolated stereo widening applied exclusively to frequencies above 180 Hz, keeping the bass foundation solid.
* **Hardware-Modeled FX:** Features TX Crunch (feedback & saturation), Erosion (2.8 kHz Ring Mod + 12-bit grit for small speakers), Punch Slam (OTT transient compression), and 0 dBFS Soft Clip.
* **Interactive Audition Interface:** A tactile central logo pad that triggers notes and drags vertically to transpose pitch in real time (±12 semitones).
* **TX81Z Phosphor LCD:** Real-time lock-free oscilloscope, integrated output peak meter, MIDI activity LED, and dynamic hover tooltips.
* **40 Curated Factory Presets + Native Preset Saving:** Instant genre-ready patches and clean in-app preset saving.
* **MIDI Expressiveness:** Full support for Pitch Bend (±2 semitones) and Mod Wheel (CC 1 dynamically opens the filter up to +200%).
* **Cross-Platform Multi-Format:** VST3, AU, CLAP, and Standalone on macOS (Universal), Windows (x64), and Linux (x64).
